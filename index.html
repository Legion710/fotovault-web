Questions

Good to know.

What is Fotovault?

A simple way for photographers to deliver their photos as a private, branded online gallery — your clients view and download their photos from one secure link.

How does the free trial work?

Sign up and you get 14 days to try Fotovault — no card required. The trial includes up to 10 GB of storage and every feature except linking additional accounts. Choose a plan whenever you're ready — the trial is once per account.

Where are my photos kept?

On South African infrastructure that we host and manage. Prefer to keep them in your own Google Drive? You can connect it and store your galleries there instead.

Is it private?

Yes. Every gallery can be password-protected with a link that expires, and your account sign-in uses hashed passwords plus a one-time email code.

Do you add VAT?

No. Fotovault isn't a registered VAT vendor, so the price on the plan is the full, final price in Rand.
